Pokémon Masters EX

DeNA's mobile game (launched August 2019, renamed Masters EX for its first anniversary) pairs trainers with one Pokémon as a sync pair on the artificial island of Pasio. Teams of three pairs fight 3-on-3 in real time and unleash sync moves. Sync pairs fill roles: Strike, Tech, Support, and later Sprint and Field. Maxing a pair to 5★ unlocks its EX outfit.

Partners shown are each trainer's signature sync pair. Many trainers have extra variants such as seasonal outfits, Sygna Suits and Neo Champion versions.
