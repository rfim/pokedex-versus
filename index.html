Gen I–IX, all 1,025 species plus Mega, regional and Legends: Z-A forms. Each slot takes its colour from a classic game version.

Trainers

Gym Leaders, Elite Four, Champions, rivals, villains and every main-series protagonist, from Kanto to Paldea. Each team is maxed out for battle. Tap any Pokémon to add it to the compare view, or Compare team to send four at once.

Team rules

  • Six, fully evolved. Dashed slots are filler picks matching the trainer's speciality and era.
  • Exactly one power-up. One Mega, Gigantamax, Tera ace, sub-legendary, mythical, Paradox or Ultra Beast per team.
  • Every starter, a different player. Each starter is given to one player in its Mega, Gigantamax or Tera form.
  • Every rare Pokémon recruited. Each mythical, Ultra Beast, Paradox and non-box legendary joins at least one team.
  • Secret boss. Box and super legendaries (Arceus, Mewtwo, Hoopa…) wait as a seventh Pokémon, in their strongest form.
  • Lv. 200, maxed. Team power counts every stat with 31 IVs, 252 EVs and a boosting nature.
